STEP 4 · DISCOVER THE PATTERN 🔎

Look at the sentences

I usually take the bus.
She always rides her bike.
They sometimes travel by plane.
We never take a helicopter.
I am always early. · She is usually on time. · They are never late.
Rule 1
Subject + adverb + main verb
I usually take the bus.
Rule 2 · with "to be"
Subject + am/is/are + adverb
She is usually on time.
